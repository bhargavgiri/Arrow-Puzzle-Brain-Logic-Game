1Information we collect ourselves

None. The Game does not send any personal information to us. We have no player accounts and no game servers.

2Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your device so the Game can work, and never leaves it:

  • Game progress: unlocked levels, stars, scores, daily challenge results and chapter rewards
  • Hints, undos and your daily reward streak
  • Settings: sound, vibration and notification preferences
  • Your advertising consent choice

Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes all of it. We have no copy and cannot restore it.

3Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows banner, native, interstitial, rewarded and app open ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and uses the Google User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it. These Google services may automatically collect and process:

We do not receive, see or store this data. It is handled by Google as described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Watching a rewarded video is always your choice and only starts when you tap its button.

4Notifications

The Game can show reminders, for example that your daily reward or daily challenge is ready. These are local notifications scheduled on your own device. They are not sent from a server and involve no data collection. Android asks for your permission first, and you can turn reminders off at any time in the Game's Settings or in your device settings.

5Rating and sharing

  • Rating: the Game may show Google Play's own in-app review card. Anything you submit there goes to Google Play, not to us.
  • Sharing: the Share button opens your device's share sheet with a short message and a link to the Game. We do not see what you share or with whom.

7Your choices and rights

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: on first launch you are asked whether you consent to personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy Settings inside the Game. Where consent is required it is the legal basis for personalised advertising; non-personalised ads rely on legitimate interest in keeping a free game running.
  • United States (including California): we do not sell personal information. You can limit ad personalisation in your device settings as described below.
  • Everyone: on Android open Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation.
  • To request access to or deletion of data held by Google, use your Google account controls at myaccount.google.com.

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we hold no personal data about you, we can usually only point you to the controls above, but you are welcome to contact us with any request.

8Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help.

9Data sharing, sale and transfers

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone other than the advertising service described in section 3. Google may process data on servers outside your country, under the safeguards described in its own policy.

10Security and retention

Data handled by Google is encrypted in transit. We keep no personal data on servers of our own, so there is nothing for us to retain. Data stored on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the Game.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or the law changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.
